## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for fast-paced retail floor operations, inventory intake, and price verification under dynamic lighting conditions. It embodies a clean, highly functional utilitarian aesthetic that minimizes cognitive load while maximizing immediate data clarity.

The visual direction balances institutional reliability with swift ergonomic handling. The interface serves floor staff who transition rapidly between scanning handheld items, cross-referencing shelf tags, and assisting customers. Visual hierarchy favors high-density readability, immediate validation states, and zero-latency tap targets designed for one-handed operation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for rapid visual parsing in retail and stockroom environments where glare and rapid physical movement are constant factors.

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal blue provides clear anchor points for active workflows, primary actions, and scanner trigger surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#16A34A` / `#15803D`)**: Emerald green delivers unambiguous positive confirmation for matched prices, active promotions, and verified barcodes. Light container fills (`#DCFCE7`) provide immediate visual separation without overpowering text.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`)**: Deep teal accents differentiate secondary operational indicators such as inventory levels, shelf batch numbers, or category badges.
- **Neutral & Surface Hierarchy**: Text uses `#0F172A` and `#334155` for high-contrast contrast against pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) and a cool operational canvas background (`#F8FAFC`). Attention and pending alerts leverage an amber accent (`#D97706`).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes tabular clarity and glanceability from arm's length. Inter is utilized throughout all levels, taking advantage of its tall x-height and clear distinction between similar glyphs.

- **Monetary Values**: The `display-price` token uses an ultra-bold weight (`900`) with tighter letter tracking to present currency symbols and numerical digits as a unified, instantly identifiable shape.
- **Identifiers & Barcodes**: The `label-code` level applies letter spacing and medium-heavy weighting to ensure numeric barcode strings and SKUs remain scannable to the human eye without confusion between numbers like 0, 8, and 6.
- **Hierarchy Separation**: Product descriptions adhere to regular or medium weights (`body-md`), preserving maximum contrast for numeric price and quantity blocks.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a dense, fluid single-column model optimized for vertical handheld devices (360px to 430px wide viewports) and compact retail tablets. 

Outer screen margins stay fixed at `0.75rem` (`margin-mobile`) to conserve horizontal screen real estate for barcode readouts and pricing tables. Component internals rely on strict multiples of `0.25rem`, utilizing compact vertical padding to keep essential verification metrics above the bottom thumb reach zone. Fixed lower navigation and scan action bars are decoupled from standard scrolling containers to maintain uninterrupted tactile access.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed primarily through flat, low-contrast structural outlines rather than heavy atmospheric shadows, ensuring clarity on lower-tier retail mobile displays.

- **Base Layer**: Background canvas sits at `#F8FAFC`.
- **Card Surfaces**: Key content areas (scanned item detail, price comparison matrices) use crisp `#FFFFFF` fills bounded by a subtle `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Active Elevation**: Floating action panels (such as the camera scan trigger or rapid manual entry drawer) utilize a crisp ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, supplemented by an explicit border to maintain definition against bright shelf lighting.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness profile (`roundedness: 2`), translating to an 8px (`0.5rem`) base corner radius across cards, form inputs, and operational buttons. 

This standard creates a modern, clean boundary that clearly delineates interactive components without wasting boundary surface area. Compact status chips and tag pills scale up to `rounded-lg` (16px) to communicate independent status indicators, while physical touch buttons maintain stable 8px bounds to maximize clickable hit-box coverage.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Scan / Confirm)**: Solid `#2563EB` background with `#FFFFFF` bold text. Minimum touch height of 52px for rapid physical actuation. Border-radius `0.5rem`.
- **Success Action (Approve / Save)**: Solid `#16A34A` background with crisp white typography.
- **Secondary Action**: Border of `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, transparent fill, `#0F172A` text.
- **Pressed State**: Active states must show immediate luminance drop (`filter: brightness(0.92)`) with no transition delay to provide tactile feedback on rapid touches.

### Price Cards & Display Blocks
- Contained in `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `0.5rem` radius.
- Includes a dedicated emerald success banner (`#DCFCE7` fill with `#15803D` typography) for verified price status.
- Numerals are set using `display-price` or `display-price-mobile` tokens to allow verification without repositioning the mobile terminal.

### Input Fields & Manual Code Search
- Height: 48px to 52px.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with a `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1`.
- Focus State: Border switches directly to `#2563EB` with an outline ring of `3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. Font is fixed to tabular Inter numbers.
- Integrated clear and barcode scanner shortcuts are pinned directly to the right edge with a minimum 44px tap width.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-lg`) tags with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Standard Tag: Neutral background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`.
- Promo / Discount Badge: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
- Match Status: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`.

### List Rows & History
- Compact horizontal rows separated by `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- High vertical centering with primary item titles truncated safely at line two, while SKU codes and monetary balances remain anchored to the right column with uninterrupted width.
